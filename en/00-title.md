# Mother of Lemmatizers

*One lemmatizer for every language in the Glottolog list: it recognises the language of each text segment, lemmatizes with curated grammar rules where they exist, and, for a language it has never seen, finds its relatives and derives a provisional grammar from them using the regularities of language change.*

- established knowledge: curated rules, treebanks, documented grammars
- inferred: transferred, induced or reconstructed, always carries a confidence
